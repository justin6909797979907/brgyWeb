Basic Needs / Components
1. Header Section
- Barangay Logo / Seal
- Barangay Name and Location
- Navigation Menu (Home, About, Services, Contact, Announcements, etc.)

2. Hero Banner / Welcome Message
- Eye-catching image of the barangay or landmark
- Short welcome message from the Barangay Captain or Council

3. About the Barangay
- Brief history or background
- Mission and vision
- Organizational structure (Captain, Kagawads, SK Chair, etc.)

4. News & Announcements
- Latest barangay updates
- Events, activities, schedules
- Emergency alerts or advisories

5. Barangay Services
- Certificates (e.g., Barangay Clearance, Indigency, Residency)
- Health services (e.g., medical mission, vaccination)
- Peace and order programs
- Waste management and cleanliness programs

6. Online Forms or Appointments
- Downloadable forms (PDF/Word)
- Online request or scheduling for documents
- Contact form for inquiries

7. Photo & Video Gallery
- Events and community activities
- Barangay improvements or projects

8. Contact Information
- Barangay Hall address
- Phone number
- Email address
- Office hours

9. Map & Directions
- Google Map embed or simple map graphic

10. Footer
- Quick links
- Social media links (if any)
- Copyright info

✅ Why You Might Want a Forum:
- To encourage community engagement (residents can ask questions, share concerns, or give suggestions)
- For feedback on barangay projects, policies, or events
- As a place for residents to connect (like a digital tambayan)
- For public discussions (e.g., disaster preparedness, neighborhood watch, etc.)
